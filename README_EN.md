# 📊 Electric Vehicles (EV) – Exploratory Data Analysis

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of technical and commercial performance data of electric vehicle (EV) models between 2020 and 2026

## 📋 About the Project

Comprehensive analysis of the global EV market focusing on:

* Technical performance data (price, battery capacity, range, horsepower)
* Categorical market structure (brand, body type, drive type, market segment)
* Relationships between technical and commercial metrics
* Brand comparisons and sales analysis
* Business insights for the global EV market

## 📦 Dataset

**Data Source:** [EV Market Analytics - Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/code/lukhilaksh/ev-market-analytics-76-beats)

**2,000 observations** with 24 columns, where each row represents one brand's model for a given year and variant (base, standard, long range, premium, performance)

**Brands by Country of Origin:**

* **Germany:** Audi, BMW, Mercedes, Porsche, Volkswagen
* **USA:** Fisker, Ford, GM/Chevrolet, Lucid, Rivian, Tesla
* **Japan:** Honda, Toyota
* **South Korea:** Hyundai, Kia
* **China:** BYD, NIO, Xiaomi
* **Sweden:** Polestar, Volvo

**Key Metrics:** Price (USD), Battery Capacity (kWh), Range (miles), Horsepower (hp), Drive Type, Body Type, Market Segment, Annual Sales Units, Customer Rating

## 🔍 Key Findings

### 📈 Key Business Insights

✅ **Battery capacity** and **vehicle range** show a very strong positive relationship (r = 0.978)  
✅ **Horsepower** influences **vehicle price** more strongly (r = 0.765) than battery capacity does (r = 0.164)  
✅ Vehicles with **longer range** tend to receive **higher customer ratings** (r = 0.668)  
✅ **Lucid, Porsche, Mercedes** lead in average price level; **Volkswagen, Tesla, BYD** lead in sales volume  
✅ The **Luxury** segment has the highest average price, yet the **Budget** segment shows the highest average sales  
✅ Extreme values in price, power, and rating are largely driven by **premium and high-performance vehicles**, and were not treated as data errors  

**Key Insight:** A high price does not automatically mean high sales. Technical specifications, brand positioning, and market segment together determine price, sales performance, and customer satisfaction in the EV market.
